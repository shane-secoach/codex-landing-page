App concept for growers

Charles Dowding’s Sowing Timeline

A simple app idea to plan sowing at the right time and avoid missing key windows in the growing season.

The process is physical, seasonal, and easy to get wrong when life gets busy. This app concept turns sowing knowledge into clear weekly actions: what to sow, what to buy, and how to protect young plants.

View core features Try timeline planner

What the app helps you manage

Sowing timing

Know the right sowing window for each crop based on season, climate, and growing method.

Seed buying reminders

Get advance prompts so seeds are bought before key sowing dates, not after them.

Materials checklist

Track what you need to make sowing happen: trays, compost, labels, watering kit, and workspace prep.

Seedling protection

Plan protection tasks with mesh or fleece so seedlings stay safe from pests and cold snaps.

Example timeline flow

“4-6 weeks before sowing: check stock, order missing seeds, and prepare compost and modules.”

“Sowing week: complete sowing tasks, add reminders for watering, and set alerts for mesh or fleece if weather risk rises.”

Mini timeline planner

Pick a stage and get instant tasks so the page actually helps you plan the physical sowing process.
